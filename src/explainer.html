Your AI assistant, with the FOI Act on its desk

FOI Navigator is a connector: an add-on you plug into Claude, Copilot or ChatGPT. Once it's connected, your assistant stops answering FOI questions from memory. It looks them up in the current FOI Act 1982 and OAIC Guidelines, works out deadlines with a calculator, and drafts letters from proper templates. Every answer cites s 15(5)(b) or [3.247].

The idea

A connector is a specialist your assistant can phone

A general AI assistant knows a bit about everything, and can be confidently wrong about the details that matter in FOI: which day the clock starts, whether s 15AA still works after a deemed refusal, what a s 29 notice must contain. A connector gives the assistant a small set of tools it can call when a question needs them, and the tools answer from authoritative sources.

The technical name is the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It is an open standard, like USB for AI tools, so the same connector works in Claude, Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT and others. You don't need to know anything about it to use the connector.

On its own, your AI assistant is

Fluent, but working from memory

It writes well and explains things in plain English. But its knowledge of the FOI Act comes from general training. That can be out of date, and it can be confidently wrong on the details.

FOI Navigator is like

The FOI team's reference shelf and calculators

The current Act and Guidelines, the deadline calculator, the exemption checklists and the template library. Your assistant consults them on your behalf.

You remain

The decision-maker

Only an officer authorised under s 23 decides. The connector gives guidance and drafts; the findings and reasons are yours.

The case file

Dates are calculated, never guessed

The connector doesn't keep a record of your requests. Instead, each tool hands your assistant a small case file: the receipt date and a list of procedural events, with no names and no document content. Your assistant passes it back on the next call. When something happens, such as an extension, a consultation or a charge, it adds an event and the calculator recomputes every statutory date.

Try it. Record events below and watch the due date and the case file change. This uses the same rules as the connector: calendar days from the day after receipt, extensions, inclusive clock-stops, and weekends rolled forward [3.247].

    What goes where
    
      
    The connector never sees your documents
    
      
    This was a deliberate design choice. FOI Navigator holds only published law, guidance and calculators. It needs procedural facts ("received 2 November, third party is a business") and general descriptions ("staff names in emails"). It never needs the applicant's identity or the documents themselves.

    Tools
    
      
    Eleven tools, all read-only
    
      
    You don't call these yourself. Your assistant chooses them based on what you ask. It's still useful to know what is in the toolkit, so you know what to ask for.
    
    
    
    
    Starting and tracking a request
    
    
    
      
    foi_start_request
    Checks validity against s 15(2), flags transfer and personal-information issues, and creates the case file with acknowledgement and decision dates.
    
      
    foi_calculate_deadlines
    Recalculates every statutory date after extensions, clock-stops, decisions and access, including review windows and the disclosure log.
    
      
    foi_process_map
    Shows all 24 steps across the five stages, for officers or line areas.
    
      
    foi_step_guidance
    The checklist, common errors and quoted law for one step, tailored to your case file.
    
    
    
    
    Doing the analysis
    
    
    
      
    foi_exemption_guide
    Elements, carve-outs and line-area questions for any exemption from s 33 to s 47J, with the section text.
    
      
    foi_public_interest_test
    A worksheet for s 11A(5) that flags s 11B(4) irrelevant factors, such as "embarrassing" or "could be misunderstood".
    
      
    foi_estimate_workload
    Scales a timed sample up to the whole request by s 24AA(2) activity, as evidence for a practical refusal.
    
      
    foi_estimate_charges
    Itemises charges under the Charges Regulations and works out the maximum deposit. It refuses to charge where the documents contain the applicant's personal information.
    
    
    
    
    Writing and reading
    
    
    
      
    foi_draft_notice
    17 templates with mandatory content built in, from the acknowledgement to the statement of reasons and the s 24AB, s 29 and consultation letters.
    
      
    search
    Finds the right section or paragraph by meaning or keyword, for example "can we redact junior staff names" returns [3.57] and [6.146].
    
      
    fetch
    Reads a whole provision or paragraph by citation, such as "s 24AB", "[3.247]" or "Part 6".

    Questions people ask
    
      
    Before you rely on it
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Is this legal advice? Does it make the decision?
    No. It is guidance and drafting support. The decision is made by an officer authorised under s 23. The Guidelines are clear that a draft prepared by someone else must be considered and personally endorsed by the decision-maker [3.298].
    
      
    How current is the law?
    The Act is compilation __ACT_ID__, in force from __ACT_IN_FORCE__. The Guidelines are the versions published by the OAIC; each part's version number is recorded below, and some parts are older (Part 14 is still v1.7 from 2022). The sources can be refreshed with a single rebuild when the OAIC updates them.
    
      
    Does the deadline calculator know public holidays?
    No. It counts calendar days and moves a deadline that lands on a weekend to the next business day. If a deadline falls on a public holiday in your state, it also moves to the next business day [3.247]. Every timeline reminds you to check this.
    
      
    Can the AI just make up a section number?
    It is much less likely to. Paragraph numbers and section text come back from the connector's search and fetch tools, and every citation in the built-in checklists is automatically tested against the bundled Act and Guidelines. Still check citations in anything you send out.
    
      
    Which jurisdictions does it cover?
    Commonwealth only, under the FOI Act 1982. It is built so that a state or territory scheme can be added later.
    
      
    Where does it run, and what does it log?
    On Cloudflare's network, as a public service with no sign-in. It keeps no record of your requests or case files. Standard request logging records only that a tool was called, for operating the service.
